My approach

A clear path from confusion to clarity

Therapy should be structured, collaborative, and focused on real change — not endless talking.

01

Understand

We begin with a thorough assessment of your history, current symptoms, and what you want to change.

02

Formulate

I create a clear "map" of what's keeping your difficulties going — and share it with you openly.

03

Treat

We use evidence-based tools — CBT, behavioral experiments, exposure, or medication when appropriate.

04

Sustain

You build a relapse-prevention plan and skills you can use for life — long after therapy ends.
